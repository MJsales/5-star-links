Questions

Do I need to already have a domain name?

No. If you already own one, I'll connect the site to it. If you don't, I'll help you pick and set one up — domain registration is paid directly to the registrar and usually runs about $10–15 a year.

Are there monthly fees?

Not to me — the $500 is one flat payment. Hosting for a site like this is typically free or a few dollars a month depending on what it needs, and that's paid to the host directly, not to me.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready for you to review within about a week of getting your details, then live shortly after you approve it. Bigger or more complex sites can take longer — I'll tell you upfront.

What if I want changes later?

Revisions during the build are included until you're happy with it. Changes further down the road are easy to do too — just reach out and we'll sort it out.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It's yours — the design, the content, and the domain are all in your name.
